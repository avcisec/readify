# Non-goals

The repository-foundation and UX-definition phases do not implement product features, production infrastructure, model evaluation systems, or a custom agent orchestrator.

The product MVP intentionally excludes the `Later` items in [FEATURES.md](../../FEATURES.md), notably:

- community, tutors, public content publishing, social feeds, leaderboards, and challenges;
- voice cloning/design, speaking or writing assessment, and broad multi-language guarantees;
- podcast/RSS, DOCX, bulk import, advanced PDF layout reconstruction, and handwriting OCR;
- bidirectional Anki sync and advanced grammar/production cards;
- standalone raw-audio import, which is not a currently declared MVP source type;
- TXT file upload; plain-text intake is direct paste/edit through the Import surface instead;
- autoscaling or microservice extraction before measured need;
- claims that a CEFR estimate is an official proficiency test.

Research findings do not enter scope merely because a competitor has a feature. Monetization, native mobile/offline behavior, and public content licensing also remain undecided rather than implicitly included.
